Frequently asked questions

The questions that actually decide whether to book — how a price is arrived at, what is included, and where the limits are. If yours is not here, ask.

There is no commitment attached to it, and the price is confirmed before any work starts. Nothing is booked from the form itself; the date is agreed after you have seen the number.
The form asks five things that do most of the work: how many storeys the property has, whether basement windows are included, roughly how many windows there are, the approximate square footage, and which services you want. Estimates are fine — an approximate window count is more useful than a blank. Beyond that, access matters: a flat lawn on all four sides is quicker than a deck, a steep grade or a fenced side yard the width of a ladder.
Interior and exterior glass, screens taken out and washed, sills and frames wiped down, and light track cleaning so sliders and casements run properly. See residential window cleaning for the full list. Track work is a clean, not a repair — if a roller has failed or a channel is damaged we tell you rather than clean around it.
Someone has to let us in for interior glass. For exterior-only work while you are out, say so when you ask for the quote so we can agree how that would work rather than assume it. Before we arrive it helps to clear ornaments and plants off the sills, unlock or unblock the rooms you want done, and take down any blind or curtain that will not pull clear of the frame.
Exterior work is weather-dependent, and the honest answer is that it is agreed case by case. Settle it with us when you book: some people would rather we came back the following week, others want the interior done anyway while we are there. Contrary to the old myth, rain does not undo a proper clean — it is working in it that is the problem.
