Glass a customer sees before they see you

Commercial glass is judged from the pavement. Fingerprints round a door handle, salt haze along the bottom of a display window and the grey film that builds on anything facing a road are all invisible from inside and obvious from outside. That is the difference between a shop that looks open and one that looks tired.

A commercial visit covers the same ground as a residential one — glass, frames, sills and tracks — with the schedule built around your trading hours rather than ours. Tell us the times that would work and we will tell you what we can do; we would rather say no to a slot than agree one and miss it.

How commercial glass is priced

Frontage is measured in panes and access, not in floor area. A wide single-storey shopfront with six large panes is quick. The same footage split into twenty small ones with a canopy in the way is not. Height, whether there is a ladder-safe footing, and how much of the glass is interior all move the number.

Recurring work generally settles at a lower per-visit figure than a one-off, because glass that is cleaned regularly never gets properly dirty. We will quote both so you can see the difference rather than take our word for it.
