Streak-free windows in Bowmanville

Bowmanville's historic core is full of Victorian and Edwardian houses with tall, narrow sashes, transoms and storm windows — glass that rewards a careful hand and punishes a rushed one. We do these pane by pane, and we're careful around old glazing putty and painted wood.

The downtown King Street storefronts are some of the most-looked-at glass in Clarington. Clean windows and clean awning glass make a real difference to a display, and we can work before opening so we're never in the way of customers.
